# My Project Brief

## The Questions

**Which settlements in Ikosi-Ketu, Lagos, are located more than 5 km by road from the nearest healthcare facility?**

## Why It Matters

The analysis could help identify communities with poor physical access to healthcare services. The results could support healthcare planning, identify underserved areas, and highlight locations that may require improved healthcare coverage or additional facilities.

## The Data I Need

* **Ikosi-Ketu study boundary:** Ward/administrative boundaries covering the Ikosi-Ketu study area.
* **Healthcare facilities:** Locations and types of hospitals, clinics, health centres, and other healthcare facilities.
* **Road network:** Road centerlines and classifications for calculating actual road distance.
* **Settlement locations:** Communities, neighbourhoods, populated places, and residential areas.
* **Population data:** Population distribution for estimating the number of people potentially affected.

## The Data Source

* **Administrative boundaries** – GRID3 Nigeria Operational Ward Boundaries
  [GRID3 Nigeria Ward Boundaries](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=0824aded5f5a4d39b10871c667aa8ccf&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* **Healthcare facilities** – GRID3 Nigeria Health Facilities v2.0, incorporating Nigeria Health Facility Registry data
  [GRID3 Nigeria Health Facilities v2.0](https://services3.arcgis.com/BU6Aadhn6tbBEdyk/ArcGIS/rest/services/GRID3_NGA_health_facilities_v2_0/FeatureServer/0?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* **Road network** – OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik Nigeria
  [Geofabrik Nigeria OSM Downloads](https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* **Settlement locations** – OpenStreetMap
  [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* **Population data** – WorldPop Nigeria Population v2.1
  [WorldPop Nigeria Population Downloads](https://data.worldpop.org/repo/wopr/NGA/population/v2.1/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## What I Would Build

I would build an interactive **Healthcare Accessibility Map and Dashboard for Ikosi-Ketu, Lagos**, showing healthcare facilities, roads, settlements, and areas located more than **5 km by road** from the nearest healthcare facility.

The dashboard would allow users to select an area and see:

* Nearest healthcare facility
* Road distance to the facility
* Number of underserved settlements
* Percentage of settlements beyond 5 km
* Estimated population affected
